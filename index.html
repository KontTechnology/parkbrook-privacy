Privacy Policy

Application: Parkbrook: Car Parking Puzzle (package com.kont.parkbrook)
Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Parkbrook parking puzzle game handles. It is written to match the actual behaviour of the app, not a generic template.

1. Summary

Parkbrook is played on your device. It needs no account and no sign-in, and the developer runs no servers for it. The game can optionally use Google Play Games Services for achievements, leaderboards and a cloud save (section 4); if you don't use Play Games, your progress stays only on your device. An optional daily reminder notification is scheduled on your device and sends no data (section 5). The app does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

2. Data the developer collects

None. The app has no analytics belonging to the developer. Nothing you do in the game is sent to the developer.

List of our other games. To show the "More games from us" card, the game downloads a public list of our games and their icons from our developer site (konttechnology.github.io, hosted by GitHub Pages) at most about twice a day. The request contains no personal data, no identifiers and nothing about how you play; like any web request, it reveals your IP address to the hosting provider. To hide games you already have, the game checks on your device whether our other apps are installed; this check never leaves your device. Tapping a game opens its Google Play page.

3. Data stored on your device

The game stores in its own private storage: your settings (sound, vibration, theme), your profile name and avatar (you type the name yourself), your progress (level, stars and best moves for each level, levels solved at par, daily lots cleared, your streak and best streak, cars driven out this week and last week and in total), your streak shields, your daily hint balance and how many hints you got by video today, unfinished lots so you can continue them, and which guides and tips you have seen. It also keeps simple counters used for ad timing and for showing the Pro offer only once. Your profile name is never sent to the developer. This data leaves your device only through Android's own backup (if you have enabled it for your Google account) and through the optional Play Games cloud save described below. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

4. Google Play Games Services (optional)

This game optionally uses Google Play Games Services. If you have a Play Games account on your device, the game recognizes you automatically; if you don't, or you don't sign in, the game works exactly the same and your progress stays only on your device. When you are signed in, the following is stored and processed by Google in your Play Games account: your Play Games player name and avatar (player identity), the achievements you unlock, the scores submitted to leaderboards (highest level completed and number of levels solved at par) and your cloud save (game progress and settings, including the profile name and avatar you chose in the game; your sound and vibration settings, daily hint balance, streak shields, unfinished lots, ad counters and which hand guides and tip bubbles you have seen are not included). Kont Technology does not keep this data on its own servers; we only access this game's data through Google's interfaces. You choose who can see your scores and profile in your Play Games profile settings. You can delete this data from your Play Games profile or your Google Account (myaccount.google.com). Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Daily reminder notification (optional)

Parkbrook offers an optional daily reminder notification. It is scheduled on your device and no data is sent for it. It is off until you turn it on: the game asks once, after you finish your first Daily Lot, and you can switch it on or off and choose the time in Settings (default 19:00). At most one reminder is shown per day, and only if that day's Daily Lot is not done yet. Its settings are kept in a separate file on your device and are not included in Android backup or the Play Games cloud save. You can also block the notifications at any time in Android's app settings.

6. Purchases

An optional one-time "Parkbrook Pro" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase exists, so that Pro can be restored on your devices. There are no subscriptions. The game may show Google Play's own rating window at most once, right after a cleared lot, and only after you have cleared at least 5 lots on at least 2 different days; it is never shown right after a full-screen ad. Your rating and review go directly to Google Play, and the developer sees only what Google Play shows publicly.

7. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen and on the result panel (never on the board while you play), an occasional full-screen ad after a finished lot, before the result panel (never during a lot, never in your first session and never in your first 8 lots; at most one in every 3 finished lots, at least 3 minutes apart and at most 4 per session), and an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch for one extra hint when your 3 free daily hints are used up (at most 5 a day). To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • device information (model, operating system, language, screen size),
  • ad interaction data (whether an ad was shown, viewed or clicked).

This processing is carried out by Google under its own terms. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy. Pro users see no ads at all.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in a region where consent is required, the app shows a Google-certified consent screen (Google User Messaging Platform) the first time it runs. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. If consent is not given, the game still works in full.

Controlling advertising on your device

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (menu names vary by manufacturer).

8. Permissions

Parkbrook itself declares the VIBRATE permission, used for short vibrations when you play (you can turn them off in Settings), and, for the optional daily reminder, POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13 and later; asked only when you turn the reminder on, and you can refuse) and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED (so the reminder is set again after your phone restarts). It does not request exact alarm permissions. Internet access, network state, advertising ID and Android Ad Services permissions are declared by the Google Mobile Ads SDK; the billing permission by Google Play Billing; wake lock and foreground service permissions by the included Google libraries for their background work.

9. Children

Parkbrook is a puzzle game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect any data from children. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

10. Data deletion

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request deletion of from us. To remove everything the game stored on your device, uninstall it or use Settings → Apps → Parkbrook → Storage → Clear data. Play Games data (achievements, scores, cloud save) can be deleted from your Play Games profile or your Google Account.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: konttechnology@gmail.com

Parkbrook, isteğe bağlı bir günlük hatırlatma bildirimi sunar. Bildirim cihazınızda zamanlanır ve bunun için hiçbir veri gönderilmez. 6. Satın almalar

İsteğe bağlı tek seferlik "Parkbrook Pro" satın alması tamamen Google Play tarafından işlenir. Geliştirici ödeme bilgilerinizi hiçbir zaman görmez. Uygulama yalnızca Pro'nun cihazlarınızda geri yüklenebilmesi için satın almanın var olup olmadığını Google Play'e sorar. Abonelik yoktur. Oyun, Google Play'in kendi puanlama penceresini en fazla bir kez, boşaltılan bir otoparktan hemen sonra gösterebilir; bu da yalnızca en az 2 farklı günde en az 5 otopark boşalttıktan sonra olur ve tam ekran reklamın hemen ardından asla gösterilmez. Puanınız ve yorumunuz doğrudan Google Play'e gider, geliştirici yalnızca Google Play'in herkese açık olarak gösterdiğini görür.

7. Reklamlar (Google AdMob)

Ücretsiz sürüm Google AdMob tarafından sağlanan reklamlar gösterir: ana sayfada ve sonuç panelinde bir banner (oynarken tahtada asla), biten bir otoparktan sonra, sonuç panelinden önce ara sıra tam ekran reklam (otopark sırasında, ilk oturumunuzda ve ilk 8 otoparkınızda asla; en fazla 3 otoparkta bir, en az 3 dakika arayla ve oturum başına en fazla 4) ve günlük 3 ücretsiz ipucunuz bitince bir ek ipucu için isteğe bağlı olarak izleyebileceğiniz ödüllü video (günde en fazla 5). Reklam sunmak için Google Mobile Ads SDK'sı şunları işleyebilir:

  • reklam kimliği (Android Advertising ID) ve diğer cihaz tanımlayıcıları,
  • IP adresinden türetilen yaklaşık konum,
  • cihaz bilgileri (model, işletim sistemi, dil, ekran boyutu),
  • reklam etkileşim verileri (gösterim, görüntüleme, tıklama).

Bu işleme Google tarafından kendi koşulları kapsamında yapılır: Google'ın, hizmetlerini kullanan sitelerden veya uygulamalardan gelen bilgileri nasıl kullandığı ve Google Gizlilik Politikası. Pro kullanıcılar hiç reklam görmez.
